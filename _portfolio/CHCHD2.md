## Research focus

This project aims to uncover the molecular origins of CHCHD2 amyloid fibril nucleation, investigating what initiates fibril formation. It forms part of my broader research into the Dark Proteome.

## Publication status

This research is currently awaiting approval for publication in the *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling* (JCIM).
